    04 / DATA SOVEREIGNTY & SCHEDULED BACKUP
    
          
    No servers. Your memories stay 100% yours.
    
        
    
        
    Architected for multi-decade ownership: lossless Android v2 exchange, CryptoKit encryption, security-scoped external backups, and storage hygiene.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          01 · ANDROID v2 ↔ iOS 100% LOSSLESS ZIP
          
    Lossless Android v2 ZIP Migration
    
          
    Speaks the exact pickup-portable-backup v2 schema (backup.json + media/) alongside non-intrusive ios-extensions.json for mood, weather, tags, and pins—plus automatic pinned .safetyPreRestore rollback snapshots before overwrite imports.
    
        
    

        
    
          02 · CRYPTOKIT .pickupvault ENCRYPTION
          
    AES-256-GCM Encrypted Vaults
    
          
    Seals your entire journal and media payload using CryptoKit PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation (80,000 iterations + 16-byte random salt) and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with 96-bit nonces.
    
        
    

        
    
          03 · SECURITY-SCOPED EXTERNAL FOLDER
          
    Scheduled External Backups (Files / iCloud / NAS)
    
          
    BGAppRefreshTask and foreground scheduling write SHA-256 checksummed snapshots every 6 hours, daily at 22:30, or weekly directly into your chosen Files App, iCloud Drive, or SMB NAS security-scoped bookmark—surviving 7-day sideload expiry.
    
        
    

        
    
          04 · STORAGE HYGIENE & AI ZERO-MEDIA
          
    Orphan Cleanup & Zero-Media AI Guard
    
          
    DataManagementService audits orphan files, repairs missing references, and surfaces heaviest video clips—while AI Hub enforces a pre-send plain-text disclosure guarantee with 0 bytes media uploaded.
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          Inspect the open pickup-portable-backup v2 & .pickupvault schemas
          
    Transparent portable archives you can inspect and restore decades from now.
