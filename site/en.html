     06 / INDEX FAQ
    
        
    Before you
    install.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          06.1 // Will my entries vanish if the 7-day free certificate expires?
          
    No. As long as you do not delete the app, re-signing with the same Apple ID preserves your local sandbox data intact—and linking an external backup folder in Data Vault guarantees an independent copy in Files or iCloud Drive.
    
        
    
        
    
          06.2 // Can I import a ZIP archive from Pickup for Android?
          
    Yes—100% lossless round-trip compatibility with Android v2 backup.json + media/ and legacy entry arrays, while storing iOS-specific metadata cleanly in ios-extensions.json.
    
        
    
        
    
          06.3 // Does everything work completely offline without AI?
          
    Yes. Journaling, Live Photos, red-ink Timeline, Revisit, passcode lock, and all local import/export and scheduled backup engines operate 100% offline.
    
        
    
        
    
          06.4 // Are both Obsidian Dark and Paper Light monochrome themes included?
          
    Yes. The iOS app supports Obsidian Glass and Paper Glass themes synced visually with this Swiss monochrome interface.
