     Your Words, Your Device
    
        
    Stays on-device.
    Portable anytime.
    
        
    No accounts and no cloud servers. Built with full local import/export, scheduled automatic backups, and local storage hygiene—even if you sideload for free.
    
        
    
          
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          01 / Local Import & Export
          
    Android v2 ZIP, AES-256 Vault, and Markdown Anthology.
    
          
    Export or merge/replace-import portable Android v2 .zip packages, CryptoKit PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM .pickupvault envelopes, or readable .md.zip folders. Replace imports automatically capture a pinned safety snapshot first.
    
        
    
        
    
          02 / Scheduled Auto-Backup
          
    Write automatically to Files, iCloud Drive, or your NAS.
    
          
    Run backups every 6 hours, daily at your preferred minute, weekly, on entry edits, or on background transition—complete with SHA-256 verification and snapshot rotation.
    
        
    
        
    
          03 / Storage Breakdown & Hygiene
          
    Inspect every byte and purge unreferenced media.
    
          
    Break down exact bytes across entries, photos, video, and local snapshots, detect missing media references, and reclaim orphan files in one tap.
    
        
    
        
    
          04 / Zero-Media AI Disclosure
          
    Clear pre-send confirmation; 0 bytes of media transmitted.
    
          
    Review matched count, plaintext character count, and an explicit 0-byte media guarantee before sending a single character to your chosen model.

     FAQ
    
        
    Before you begin.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          Will my memories be lost if the 7-day free sideload signature expires?
          
    No. As long as you do not delete the app, re-signing with the same Apple ID preserves your local sandbox data intact—and linking an external backup folder in Data Vault guarantees an independent copy in Files or iCloud Drive.
    
        
    
        
    
          Can I import a ZIP exported from Pickup for Android?
          
    Yes—100% lossless round-trip compatibility with Android v2 backup.json + media/ and legacy entry arrays, while storing iOS-specific metadata cleanly in ios-extensions.json.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does the app work completely offline without AI?
          
    Yes. Journaling, Live Photos, red-ink Timeline, Revisit, passcode lock, and all local import/export and scheduled backup features work 100% offline.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does it offer both dark and light pure monochrome glass styles?
          
    Yes. Both the iOS app and this website support Obsidian Dark Glass (#080808) and Paper Light Glass (#F5F4EF)—strictly black, white, and frosted glass.
